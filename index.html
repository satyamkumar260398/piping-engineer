About us

A place to learn. A place to belong.

Our mission: to help every child build a strong foundation.

Founded in 2016, Prayag Gurukul is a welcoming learning space for children in Classes 1–8 in Prayagraj. We use simple explanations, regular practice and individual guidance to make learning feel approachable.
